⚛️ Physics

Physics explores matter, energy, motion, forces, light, sound, and electricity.

Examples of Physics in Everyday Life

  • Gravity keeps us on Earth's surface.
  • Light allows us to see objects.
  • Friction helps shoes grip the ground.
  • Electricity powers many everyday devices.

🧬 Biology

Biology is the scientific study of living organisms, including plants, animals, and microorganisms.

Characteristics of Living Things

  • They use energy.
  • They grow and develop.
  • They respond to their environment.
  • They reproduce as part of their life cycles.

🧪 Chemistry

Chemistry studies matter, its properties, its composition, and how substances change.

Examples

  • Water has the chemical formula H2O.
  • Oxygen has the chemical symbol O.
  • Iron can react with oxygen and moisture to rust.

💡 Science Challenge

Why does the Moon appear bright?

The Moon reflects sunlight. It does not produce its own visible light like the Sun.

What is the basic unit of life?

The cell is the basic structural unit of life.

What is the center of an atom called?

The center of an atom is called its nucleus.
