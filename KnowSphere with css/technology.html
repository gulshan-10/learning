🖥️ Computers

Computers process information according to instructions called programs.

Main Computer Components

  • CPU — processes instructions.
  • RAM — temporarily stores working data.
  • Storage — keeps files and programs.
  • Monitor — displays visual information.
  • Keyboard — lets users enter text.

🌐 The Internet

The internet is a worldwide network that connects computers and other devices.

What Can You Do Online?

  • Learn from educational websites.
  • Communicate with other people.
  • Share files and information.
  • Watch videos and listen to music.

🤖 Artificial Intelligence

Artificial intelligence (AI) describes computer systems designed to perform tasks that often require human-like abilities.

Examples of AI

  • Speech recognition.
  • Image recognition.
  • Language translation.
  • Systems that help answer questions.
Can AI make mistakes?

Yes. AI systems can produce incorrect or misleading answers, so important information should be checked against reliable sources.

🦾 Robotics

Robotics combines engineering, electronics, and computing to build and control robots.

Where Are Robots Used?

  • Manufacturing and factories.
  • Medical research and healthcare.
  • Space exploration.
  • Warehouses and scientific laboratories.

💡 Technology Facts

What does CPU stand for?

Central Processing Unit.

Is the internet the same as the Web?

No. The internet is the underlying network. The World Wide Web is a collection of linked resources accessed through that network.

What is HTML used for?

HTML structures webpage content, including headings, paragraphs, links, tables, and forms.
