🗺️ The Seven Continents

In the commonly taught seven-continent model, Earth's land is grouped into these continents:

  1. Asia
  2. Africa
  3. North America
  4. South America
  5. Antarctica
  6. Europe
  7. Australia
Which continent has the largest population?

Asia has the largest population.

⛰️ Landforms and Climate

Common Landforms

  • Mountains — elevated areas of land.
  • Plains — broad areas of relatively flat land.
  • Plateaus — elevated areas with relatively level surfaces.
  • Valleys — low areas between hills or mountains.
  • Deserts — regions with very low precipitation.

What Is Climate?

Climate describes the long-term patterns of weather in a region, including temperature and precipitation.
